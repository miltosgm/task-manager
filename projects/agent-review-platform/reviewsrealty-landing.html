Frequently Asked Questions

Are the reviews verified?

Yes. We verify reviews through transaction history and contact information. Only genuine client reviews are published.

Is there a cost to use ReviewsRealty?

No. Finding agents and reading reviews is completely free. For agents, basic profile is free. Premium tools are €29/month.

How do I post a review?

After you work with an agent, we'll send you a review link. It takes 2 minutes to share your feedback.

Can agents respond to reviews?

Yes. Agents can respond to any review within 30 days. We encourage professional dialogue.

How do you prevent fake reviews?

We verify all reviews through transaction confirmation. Fake reviews are removed immediately.

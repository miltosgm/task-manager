# GROWTH-ONOMICS · TASK 5 · LINKEDIN TOUCH 1 & 2 SCRIPTS

**Strategy:** Warm outreach via comments on target prospects' posts (Touch 1), followed by DM after engagement (Touch 2).
**Voice:** Miltos's personal account — direct, peer-level, not salesy.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS

1. **Touch 1:** Find a recent post from the target contact. Leave the comment below on that post.
2. **Wait 3–5 days.** If they reply, respond naturally. If they don't, still proceed to Touch 2.
3. **Touch 2:** Send the DM. Reference the comment to warm the connection.
4. **Do not pitch on Touch 1 or Touch 2.** The goal of both touches is a reply, not a sale.

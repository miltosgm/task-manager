What's Inside the 7-Day Sprint

Day 1: Authority Foundation

Establish your positioning in structured formats AI can parse. Update profiles, add FAQs, create "best for" statements.

Day 2: Answer Engine Fuel

Create 5 pieces of content AI engines love: how-to guides, comparisons, decision frameworks, and case studies.

Day 3: Third-Party Validation

Get your brand mentioned on sites AI trusts: directories, Reddit, LinkedIn comments, industry publications.

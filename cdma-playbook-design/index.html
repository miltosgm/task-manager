01 Context & Challenge
Why Hospitality Needs
24/7 IT Support
Hospitality operates around the clock. A server crash at 2 AM during peak season does not wait for business hours. A Wi-Fi outage in a 300-room resort triggers hundreds of guest complaints within minutes. In hospitality, IT is not back-office — it is the guest experience.
Always-On Operations
Hotels, resorts, and venues operate 24/7/365. Front desk check-ins happen at midnight. Room service flows through POS at 3 AM. Any downtime directly impacts guest experience and revenue.
Peak Season Pressure
Occupancy surges strain networks, PMS, and payment processing. Seasonal peaks bring 2–4 devices per guest, requiring high-density Wi-Fi. The worst time for an IT failure is precisely when it is most likely to occur.
Complex Vendor Ecosystem
PMS (Opera, Mews, Protel), POS (Micros, Lightspeed), IPTV, door locks, CCTV, and VoIP. Coordinating between these systems requires deep domain knowledge and proactive management.
Staff Turnover & Onboarding
Hospitality has one of the highest staff turnover rates of any industry. Seasonal hiring means constant onboarding and offboarding — creating operational overhead and security risk.
Payment & Privacy Compliance
PCI DSS and GDPR are non-negotiable. Guest databases contain PII, payment systems run continuously, and strict network segmentation must be maintained at all times.
Mobile Coverage Gaps
Thick walls, basements, and large conference areas create signal dead zones that frustrate guests and hinder staff. Comprehensive 4G/5G DAS coverage is an increasingly expected amenity.
Support Availability
24/7
CDMA NOC, Service Desk & MDR/SOC operate year-round with no shift gaps or out-of-hours surcharges on Premium tier.
Threat Response SLA
15 min
MDR isolates active threats within 15 minutes — before attackers can move laterally through your network.
Bottom Line
  • Hospitality IT demands proactive 24/7 monitoring
  • Reactive support is not viable when guest satisfaction and revenue are on the line
  • Every minute of downtime has a direct cost measured in guest experience
All CDMA services are delivered on a white-label basis. From your guests' and staff's perspective, we operate as a seamless extension of your own IT team.

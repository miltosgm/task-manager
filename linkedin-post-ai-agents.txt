LINKEDIN POST DRAFT - AI AGENTS
================================

I spend more time talking to AI agents than my own team.

And I'm not mad about it.

(Sorry team. Stelios, you're still my #1 human.)

Here's what my AI "team" handles daily 👇

Clawdbot (my AI operator)
• Researches a prospect → gives me a 1-page brief before the call
• Scrapes 275 real estate agents from a directory → cleaned data in my inbox
• Monitors competitor pricing pages → alerts me when something changes
• Builds spreadsheets with contact info I'd spend hours collecting
• Drafts outreach emails, follow-ups, and client reports

Memorae (my AI memory)
• "Remind me to follow up with that lead Friday" → done
• Tracks every commitment I make across conversations
• Knows what I said last Tuesday better than I do

Yesterday I said:
"Scrape every real estate agency in Cyprus with their Google ratings and contact info"

6 minutes later: 275 agencies with reviews, emails, and phone numbers.

I didn't search. Didn't copy-paste. Didn't format a single cell.

This is the shift:

The best operators won't have the biggest teams.
They'll have the best AI orchestration.

Your moat isn't headcount anymore.

It's how well you delegate to machines — so your humans focus on what actually moves the needle.

Still doing research and admin yourself?

That's a choice now. Not a necessity.

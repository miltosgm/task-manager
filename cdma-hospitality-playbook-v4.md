## 01. WHY HOSPITALITY IT IS DIFFERENT

### The Stakes Are Higher

In most industries, IT downtime is an inconvenience. In hospitality, it is an operational crisis that guests experience directly. A failed PMS prevents check-ins. A down POS terminal halts food and beverage revenue. A Wi-Fi outage generates complaints and negative reviews within minutes.

Hospitality IT has three characteristics that set it apart from any other sector:

**Always-On Requirement**
Hotels operate 24 hours a day, every day of the year. There is no overnight maintenance window that does not affect a guest or a late-night arrival. IT support must match the operational rhythm of the property.

**System Interdependency**
A single network failure can cascade across PMS, POS, IPTV, door lock systems, VoIP, and digital signage simultaneously. Understanding these dependencies and designing around them requires hospitality-specific expertise.

**Compliance Complexity**
Hotels process guest payment cards (PCI DSS), collect personal data (GDPR), and increasingly fall under NIS2 obligations. Compliance is not optional and requires ongoing management.

CDMA has built its hospitality service model around these realities. Our NOC teams follow hospitality-aware maintenance schedules. Our Service Desk understands PMS, POS, and IPTV. Our cybersecurity practice covers PCI DSS scoping and GDPR guidance as standard.

Thick concrete walls, underground car parks, basement conference rooms, and large atrium spaces all create cellular dead zones. Guests complain. Staff cannot receive calls or use mobile applications. Emergency communications may be compromised in areas where signal is weak or absent.

### CDMA's Nextivity DAS Solution

**Carrier-Agnostic Coverage**

**Passive and Active DAS**

**4G and 5G Ready**
Future-proof infrastructure supporting both 4G LTE and 5G NR frequencies, ensuring your investment remains relevant as networks evolve.

**Professional Survey and Design**
Site RF surveys identify coverage gaps precisely. System design ensures every area, including pool decks, spa, and underground parking, receives adequate signal.

**Monitoring and Maintenance**
CDMA remotely monitors DAS performance and addresses signal degradation proactively before guests experience issues.

## 09. BACKUP AND DISASTER RECOVERY

### 15-Min RPO, Immutable Backups, Documented DR Runbooks, Tested Recovery

A ransomware attack, hardware failure, or natural disaster can bring hospitality operations to a standstill. When a hotel cannot check guests in or process payments, the financial and reputational damage accumulates by the hour. CDMA's backup and disaster recovery services ensure rapid recovery with documented, tested runbooks built around your specific property stack.

### Key Recovery Metrics

| Metric | CDMA Standard | Why It Matters for Hospitality |
|---|---|---|
| Recovery Point Objective (RPO) | 15 minutes | Maximum data loss window, only 15 minutes of transactions at risk |
| Recovery Time Objective (RTO) | Defined per system tier | PMS and POS are prioritised first for restoration |
| Backup Frequency | Continuous to hourly depending on criticality | Critical systems backed up continuously, others on defined schedules |
| Retention Period | Configurable, typically 30, 90, and 365 days | Meets regulatory requirements for financial and guest data |
| Offsite Replication | Encrypted offsite copy always maintained | A site-level disaster cannot destroy primary and backup simultaneously |
| Immutability | Write-once backup repositories | Ransomware cannot encrypt or delete backup data |
| Encryption | AES-256 at rest and in transit | Backup data protected against interception and theft |

### DR Runbooks

Every managed property has a documented disaster recovery runbook specific to its technology stack. Runbooks cover: ransomware attack, critical hardware failure, ISP outage, site-level disaster (fire or flood), and cloud platform outage.

Runbooks are tested at least annually, and more frequently for Premium tier clients. Each test produces a formal report with recovery time measurements, gaps identified, and remediation actions assigned.

## 12. ONBOARDING PROCESS

### Nine-Step Structured Onboarding for Single Sites and Multi-Property Groups

CDMA's structured onboarding process ensures reliable transitions from existing providers or from unmanaged environments. Every engagement follows a documented nine-step process adapted for single properties and multi-site groups.

2. **Technical Discovery** — Remote and on-site technical assessment of existing infrastructure. Network diagrams, asset inventories, and credential collection.
3. **Platform Integration** — Integration of property systems into CDMA NOC and Service Desk platforms. RMM agent deployment, monitoring configuration.
4. **Baseline and Tuning** — Alert threshold establishment, maintenance window configuration, escalation path definition. First week of monitoring with active tuning.
5. **Security Hardening** — Firewall rule review, network segmentation validation, endpoint protection deployment, backup configuration verification.
6. **Service Desk Activation** — Staff communication about new service desk contact details, ticketing procedures, and escalation paths. White-label configuration complete.
7. **Knowledge Transfer** — Documentation of property-specific procedures, vendor contacts, and escalation preferences for NOC and Service Desk teams.
8. **Go-Live** — Full service commencement. Dedicated contact point for first 30 days to address any service delivery adjustments.
9. **30-Day Review** — Service delivery review meeting. Alert volume analysis, ticket trends, and service optimisation recommendations.
